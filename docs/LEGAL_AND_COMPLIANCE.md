# Tech Live — Legal & Platform Compliance Policies

## 1. Terms of Service
By using Tech Live, creators affirm that they own or hold explicit broadcast licenses for all live video, screen captures, audio, and prerecorded media uploaded or streamed through the platform. Tech Live provides multi-destination streaming infrastructure and software development tutorial Q&A tools and does not grant third-party content licenses.

## 2. Acceptable Use Policy
Users must comply with the community guidelines, copyright rules, OAuth policies, and streaming rate limits of every destination platform (YouTube, Facebook/Meta, TikTok, Telegram, and custom RTMP servers). Automated attempts to bypass platform eligibility checks, API rate limits, copyright detection, or account suspensions are strictly prohibited and result in immediate account termination.

## 3. Privacy Policy
Tech Live collects Google Sign-In profile identifiers, stream metadata, usage telemetry, and uploaded media solely to operate live and scheduled cloud streaming. Stream keys and OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and never shared with other users. Users may export or permanently delete their account and stored media at any time from Profile -> Settings.

## 4. Refund Policy
Monthly (BDT 100) and Lifetime (BDT 1,500) plan payments are verified via payment gateway webhook or manual TRX ID review. Verified transactions eligible for refund due to technical billing errors or duplicate charges may be refunded by an administrator from the Payment Management console, which automatically reverts the associated subscription entitlement.
